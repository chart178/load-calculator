Enter your real costs once, then check a load. See profit per day and per mile after fuel, upkeep, and fixed costs.

Your truck costs

Fixed costs means truck payment, permits, and anything else you pay whether the truck moves or not, divided by days. Insurance is spread over your road days using the weeks and days per week below. Your entries are saved on this device.

Road and living expenses

Leave the box unchecked to see profit before personal expenses.

Totals

Week starts Monday. Profit before living expenses = pay minus fuel, repairs, fixed costs, insurance, and tolls. Take-home = that minus the expenses you logged.

Saved trips

Expense log
